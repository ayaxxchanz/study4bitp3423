Detailed Summary

Organised around the lecture, with the explanation behind every key point.

The central exam argument

Adopting a technology is not the same as making it work. Integration connects systems, data, processes and people; implementation needs testing and support; evaluation asks whether the change produced credible value. Financial ROI helps, but is not the only measure of success.

1 · Integrate

Choose APIs, events, data pipelines or cloud/edge deployment according to requirements.

2 · Implement

Manage compatibility, security, ownership and change through suitable rollout.

3 · Evaluate

Use baselines, KPIs, complete costs, ROI and non-financial outcomes.

Structured Exam Practice

Past-paper style: a short scenario, numbered subquestions and specific mark allocations. Answer point-by-point, not as a long essay.

PART A15 structured questionsThree practice sets of five questions. Each question: 10 marks, split into (a) and (b).

How to answer a structured question

Check the command word (state, identify, explain, discuss, justify), the number of items requested and the allocated marks. For 4 marks, aim for two distinct explained points; for 6 marks, explain three. For a case study, use the facts in the scenario. The suggested full answers, marking points and mark allocations are practice aids, not official answer schemes.
